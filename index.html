FAQ

Common questions

Is this really free?
Yes. The discovery call and written summary cost you nothing. We'd rather have a clear picture of your business before we ever talk about what comes next — and frankly, most calls don't lead anywhere, and that's fine.
Will the AI try to sell me something?
No. The agent is explicitly built not to recommend anything during the call. It only listens and asks. Any follow-up — if there is one — happens in a separate conversation with a human.
How long does it take?
Twelve to fifteen minutes typically. If you have a lot to share, it can stretch to twenty. You can end the call at any point and you'll still get a summary of what was covered.
What happens to my information?
The transcript is stored privately and used only to produce your summary. It is not shared, sold, or used to train any AI model. You can request deletion at any time.
What if I don't have time right now?
No problem — just bookmark this page and come back when you do. The link doesn't expire and the call can be done from any device with a microphone.
Does this work on my phone?
Best on a laptop or desktop using Chrome or Edge. Mobile works but speech recognition is more reliable on desktop browsers.
