Recent work

A WhatsApp ordering bot for a meat delivery business.

A real example of what an AI roadmap looks like once it's built. Names withheld for client confidentiality.

Industry Food & delivery
Build time ~1 week
Stack WhatsApp · AI · automation

The problem

The owner came to us with a familiar list of operational frustrations:

  • No clear tracking of orders
  • Invoices written manually
  • Manual order processing
  • No visibility on performance
  • Stock-outs and overstocking
  • The whole system depended on the owner

What we built

An AI-powered WhatsApp ordering bot that customers chat with directly to place orders. The bot understands natural language, confirms orders, and generates professional invoices automatically — all within seconds. A lightweight back end captures every order in real time, and a live dashboard gives the owner a clear view of business performance at any moment.

The outcome

  • Every order tracked automatically — nothing lost, nothing forgotten
  • Instant professional invoices generated and stored automatically
  • Hands-off order processing — the bot handles clarification and confirmation
  • Live dashboard showing revenue, top products, and order volume at a glance
  • Smarter buying with visibility into what's actually selling
  • Owner freed up to focus on the business instead of running the admin

This is the kind of practical, focused automation a bespoke roadmap identifies — solving a specific pain point with the right tools, at a cost the business can justify.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the call really free?+
Yes — completely free, with no obligation to proceed. The bespoke roadmap that follows is a fixed £350 if you choose to commission it. We're transparent about that upfront so you can decide before you start. Most calls don't lead to a paid roadmap, and that's completely fine.
What does the £350 actually cover?+
A human consultant reviewing your full transcript, identifying where AI could genuinely help your business, and writing a tailored roadmap with a clear starting point. It's the same kind of analysis a consultancy would charge several thousand for — we keep it focused, useful, and affordable for SMBs.
Will the AI try to sell me something?+
No. The agent is explicitly built not to recommend anything during the call. It only listens, asks, and gently prompts you to think about which tasks could potentially be automated — without telling you the answer. Recommendations come later, in writing, after a human has reviewed your transcript.
When do I get my roadmap?+
Within 48 hours of the call ending. You'll get a brief acknowledgement email immediately after the call, and the full bespoke roadmap follows shortly after — usually next business day.
How long does the call take?+
Twelve to fifteen minutes typically. If you have a lot to share, it can stretch to twenty. You can end the call at any point and we'll still produce your roadmap based on what was covered.
What happens to my information?+
Your transcript is stored privately and used only to produce your roadmap. It's never shared, sold, or used to train any AI model. You can request deletion at any time by replying to the roadmap email.
Does this work on my phone?+
Best on a laptop or desktop using Chrome or Edge. Mobile works but speech recognition is more reliable on desktop browsers. There's also a text fallback if the microphone gives you trouble.
